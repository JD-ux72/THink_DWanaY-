# THink DWanaY 🧠📊

## The Way of the Attuned Data Scientist

> **“Data is memory made visible.”**

I am a Data Scientist walking a road I did not create.
There are people who walked it before me.
There are people walking it beside me.
And there are people who will walk it after me.
**THink DWanaY** is my way of leaving something behind on that road.
Not a map to tell you where to go.
Not a promise that I have found all the answers.
But the things I have learned while walking.
The questions that changed the way I saw.
The lessons that took time to understand.
The mistakes that taught me more than success ever could.

And the small pieces of wisdom I wish someone had placed in my hands when I began.

I am taking you through **my journey of Data Science itself** — and where I find something worth carrying forward, I hand it to you.
Because Data Science is not only a journey of learning how to work with data.
It is a journey of learning how to **gaze**.

And perhaps the greatest thing a Data Scientist can collect along the way is not knowledge alone—
but **wisdom about what to do with it.**
